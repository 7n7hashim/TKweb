### Names

Blu's posts mostly describe developments instead of naming them. The site uses a proper name only
where Blu itself published one:

- **Emaar Apartments** — printed large on Blu's own flyer, and in its hashtag `#EmaarApartments`.
  (Unrelated to the Dubai developer Emaar Properties; the flyer simply uses the name.)
- **Wavecrest Residency** — Blu's TikTok names it (`#WavecrestResidency`, with Blu's number), the
  renders Blu posted for "2nd Avenue Pinnacle" carry the developer's faint "Wavecrest" watermark,
  and third-party portals list Wavecrest Residency on 2nd Avenue, Nyali, near Voyager.

The other six use the headline Blu gave them in its 6 June round-up post. **Confirm all eight
names with Blu before launch.** One more lead: the Modern Luxury Living render shows a sign that
reads "ANWAARY RESIDENCE" (cropped out; not used), possibly Blu's "Ammary" highlight.

## Images

All 35 files here are from Blu's posts: the logo post (1080×1080), the profile picture (150×150)
and 33 slides of developers' **renders** (CGI artist's impressions of mostly unbuilt buildings),
at 1080 px (1920 px for one slide: `collection-4`, the hero). `optimize-images.mjs` crops each
one clear of Blu's flyer overlays (price panel, logo, phone), Instagram labels ("LOUNGE",
"DINING"), the Modern Luxury Living sign, and a stray logo on `skyline-4`; every crop was checked
on a contact sheet. **Every `alt` on the site says "Artist's impression … the developer's
render, posted by Blu Real Estate"; no render is ever called a photograph.**

Known: the Wavecrest renders (`collection-4` hero, `avenue-1`) carry the developer's faint
"Wavecrest" watermark across the façade. It is left in place, not retouched; the hero strip
names the building it shows.

The homepage build scrub (`brand_assets/build-frames/`) is stock construction footage inherited
from earlier builds and labelled as stock.
